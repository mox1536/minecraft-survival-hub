Home Building Mobs Enchantments

1. Passive Mobs

Passive mobs generally do not attack players.

Cows

  • Drop raw beef and leather when killed.
  • Can be bred using wheat.
  • Useful for food and leather supplies.

Sheep

  • Drop wool and mutton.
  • Can be sheared for wool.
  • Can be bred using wheat.

Villagers

  • Offer trades using emeralds and other items.
  • Can provide enchanted books through librarian trades.
  • Different professions offer different trades.

2. Hostile Mobs

Hostile mobs can attack players under their relevant spawning and aggression conditions.

Zombies

  • Burn in sunlight when exposed, unless protected or affected by special conditions.
  • Can drop rotten flesh.
  • Use a sword or another suitable weapon to fight them.

Skeletons

  • Attack with bows.
  • Can drop bones and arrows.
  • Use cover to avoid incoming arrows.

Creepers

  • Approach players and explode when close enough.
  • Can drop gunpowder.
  • Keep your distance and move away when they begin their explosion countdown.

3. Nether Mobs

Blazes

  • Found in Nether fortresses.
  • Drop blaze rods when killed.
  • Blaze rods are useful for brewing and progression.

Piglins

  • Normally become hostile when players open containers or mine gold-related blocks nearby.
  • Wear at least one piece of gold armor to help avoid ordinary piglin hostility.
  • Can barter when given gold ingots.

4. End Mobs

Endermen

  • Can drop ender pearls.
  • Become hostile when provoked, such as by looking directly at their face in the usual circumstances.
  • Water damages them, and they can teleport away.

Ender Dragon

  • The main boss of the End dimension.
  • Destroy the End crystals to prevent them from healing the dragon.
  • Use a bow against the dragon while it is flying.

5. General Mob Tips

  • Carry a shield when preparing for combat.
  • Bring food, armor, and suitable weapons.
  • Learn each mob's behavior before fighting it.
  • Remember that drops and mechanics may vary by edition and game version.
