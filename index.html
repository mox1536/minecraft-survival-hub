Survival Tips

day 1

  • Tools gathering
  1. Break 3 Oak logs
  2. Dig Down until you reach stone
  3. Mine 7 stones and craft stone pickaxe, axe, and shovel
  4. Mine 24 more stones and craft 3 furnaces
  5. Collect 20 oak logs then craft one smoker and the rest of the logs craft wood planks
  • Food gathering and first night survival
  1. Collect 27-33 raw beef/raw mutton at least
  2. collect 3 wools with the same color to craft bed
  3. build house or shelter, and put inside it : crafting table, 2 furnaces with the smoker, bed, and double chest
  4. use your wood planks/charcoal to cook your raw food

Day 2

  • Gearing up and entering the nether
  1. Go and find cave/dig staricase down outside your house/dig in 2x1 straight down but with 124 ladders
  2. if you find cave, collect 42 raw iron, and 5 coals
  3. go back home and smelt one furnace 5 raw iron, while the other one smelt 3 cobblestones
  4. smelt the 3 stones and make blast furnace with 3 smooth stones and 5 iron and one of your 2 furnaces
  5. smelt all of the rest of your iron, then craft iron boots , helmet , leggings , chestplate , iron pickaxe , axe , shovel , sword , shield and a bucket
  6. after that, find a lava pool by running around in the overworld, or inside caves in y level -54
  7. Use the water bucket to pour it on the lava, or use the speedrun method shown below:

Day 3

  • The Nether
  1. Find fortress , collect about 8 blazerods
  2. Find piglins or warpped forest and kill enderman using boats and collect 16 ender pearls and extra 2 (optional)
  3. Don't forget about taking nether wartin the fortress

End Preparation

  • How to prepare for the ender dragon fight
  1. after you finish at the nether, head to the overworld. Collect 40 raw beef and cook them, brew potions if needed, craft bow and buy arrows from the fletcher villager
  2. craft eye of ender and locate the stronghold
  3. once you find the stronghold, search for the end portal, and then fill the needed eyes of ender in the portal frames
  4. enter the end head to the obsidian pilleers
  5. break all the end crystals then use the bow to attack the dragon
  6. when it goes down, attack it untill it flies again.
  7. repeat the process until it dies

And that is how you beat the game
