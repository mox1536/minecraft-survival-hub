1. Starter House

A good starter house protects you from hostile mobs and gives you a place to store your resources.

Materials

  • Oak logs and oak planks
  • Cobblestone for the foundation
  • Glass for windows
  • Torches for lighting
  • A door, bed, and crafting table

Building Steps

  1. Choose a flat area near trees and water.
  2. Build a rectangular foundation using cobblestone.
  3. Place logs at the corners and connect them with planks.
  4. Build the walls and leave spaces for windows and a door.
  5. Add a roof using stairs or slabs.
  6. Place torches inside and around the entrance.
  7. Add a bed, chests, and crafting stations.

2. Storage Room

A storage room keeps your materials organized and makes crafting easier.

  1. Choose a room near your crafting area.
  2. Place chests in rows along the walls.
  3. Group blocks, ores, food, tools, and mob drops.
  4. Use signs or item frames to identify categories.
  5. Leave enough space to open every chest.

3. Basic Crop Farm

Grow food so you have a reliable supply for survival.

  1. Find a water source and prepare farmland with a hoe.
  2. Plant wheat seeds, carrots, or potatoes.
  3. Keep farmland hydrated with nearby water.
  4. Place torches if you want the farm lit at night.
  5. Harvest mature crops and replant them.

4. Defensive Walls

  • Build walls around your base to guide mob movement.
  • Use fences or walls around dangerous drops.
  • Add gates where you need entrances.
  • Light up the surrounding area.

5. Building Tips

  • Combine different blocks to add texture.
  • Use stairs and slabs to create detailed roofs.
  • Keep paths between important buildings.
  • Test your design in a creative world first.
