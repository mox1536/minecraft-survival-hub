1. What Are Enchantments?

Enchantments improve equipment by adding special abilities or increasing its effectiveness.

How to Get Enchantments

  • Use an enchanting table with experience levels and lapis lazuli.
  • Combine equipment or enchanted books using an anvil.
  • Find enchanted books in loot and other sources.
  • Trade with librarians for available enchanted books.

2. Sword Enchantments

Sharpness

Increases damage against many targets. Useful as a general-purpose sword enchantment.

Looting

Can increase the quantity of certain mob drops and improve some drop chances.

Unbreaking

Reduces how often durability is consumed, helping equipment last longer.

Mending

Uses collected experience to repair damaged equipment instead of adding that experience to the player's experience bar.

3. Armor Enchantments

Protection

Reduces many types of incoming damage. A useful choice for general survival.

Feather Falling

Reduces fall damage when applied to boots.

Respiration

Extends the time you can breathe underwater when applied to a helmet.

Depth Strider

Improves underwater movement when applied to boots.

4. Pickaxe Enchantments

Efficiency

Increases mining speed for suitable blocks.

Fortune

Can increase drops from certain blocks, including many ore blocks.

Silk Touch

Allows certain blocks to drop themselves instead of their usual drops.

5. Bow Enchantments

Power

Increases the damage dealt by arrows.

Infinity

Allows a bow to fire normal arrows without consuming them, provided you have at least one normal arrow in your inventory.

Flame

Sets targets hit by arrows on fire, subject to the usual game mechanics.

6. Useful Enchantment Combinations

Mining Pickaxe

  • Efficiency
  • Unbreaking
  • Fortune or Silk Touch
  • Mending

Survival Armor

  • Protection
  • Unbreaking
  • Mending
  • Feather Falling on boots

Combat Sword

  • Sharpness
  • Looting
  • Unbreaking
  • Mending

7. Important Notes

  • Some enchantments cannot normally be combined on the same item, such as Fortune and Silk Touch.
  • Check the enchantment level and equipment compatibility before using an anvil.
  • Exact mechanics and availability may differ between Java Edition, Bedrock Edition, and game versions.
